# Song licenses

Most pieces in this folder are public-domain musical works, engraved for the
[Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/) and released there as public
domain. Ivory converted those MIDI files into its own note format. Titles and
engravers are stored on each song as `attribution`.

These three files are a conversion of a Mutopia engraving that is **not**
public domain. They stay under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5:

- `moonlight-1.json`
- `moonlight-2.json`
- `moonlight-3.json`

Engraving by Stewart Holmes, after the 1908 Berners edition of Beethoven’s
Sonata Op. 27 No. 2. Beethoven’s composition is public domain. If you modify
these three JSON files, keep the same license and attribution.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/
